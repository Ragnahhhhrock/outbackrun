TYPOGRAPHY

Title Font

OUTBACK RUN

Heavy, bold, spaced. Uses default system bold with letter-spacing. Gold color with brown shadow.

HUD Font

TIME 60.0 | SPD 240

Monospace for that arcade machine feel. Cyan for time, yellow for speed, magenta for gear.

Body / UI Font

The Nullarbor Straight — Easy | Coastal | 16:00 par

Clean monospace at 14-16px. White on dark panels with gold borders.

Pixel Scale

Internal resolution: 320 x 240
Scaled via CSS with image-rendering: pixelated

ROAD SIGNS

Australian road signs are iconic — diamond warning signs, red-ringed speed limits, and the unmistakable kangaroo warning. All signs are drawn at 32x40px and scaled in-engine.

ANIMALS & HAZARDS

The Australian outback is full of wildlife that doesn't care about your high score. All sprites are drawn at low resolution and scaled.

SCENERY & PROPS

From termite mounds to dead trees, the scenery tells you exactly where you are.

CAR DESIGN

The player vehicle is a classic Australian muscle car — boxy, red, with a hint of 1970s Falcon/Commodore DNA. Drawn at 40x20px.

UI ELEMENTS

Control Layout (Mobile)

Steering wheel on left (touch-drag to steer)
Gas / Brake pedals on right
Gear toggle (LO/HI) floating center-right
HUD across top: TIME | SPEED | GEAR | DIST

Screen States

TITLE: Gold logo, route select buttons
PLAYING: Road view + HUD + controls
GAME OVER: Dark overlay, result text, retry button
ROUTE SELECT: 5 route cards with difficulty/time

ROUTE VISUAL IDENTITY

The Nullarbor Straight

Flat, endless, hypnotic. Pale sand, sparse dead trees, the occasional road train. The easiest route — long straights, gentle curves.

The Red Centre Run

Deep red dirt, termite mounds, dust storms. Kangaroo hazards spike at dusk. Medium curves, high speed potential.

The Great Divide

Eucalyptus forests, mountain switchbacks, black ice at dawn. Tight curves demand drifting. Green and grey palette.

The Top End Trek

Tropical greens, monsoon rain, flooded creek crossings. Palm trees, crocodile warning signs. Hardest route — hydroplaning physics.

The Southern Coast

Coastal curves, urban traffic near cities, police radar zones. Roundabouts replace intersections. Most varied terrain.
